Questions

Good to know.

Who is RE:BUILD coaching for?

Busy parents over 35 who want to get stronger, fitter and feel like themselves again, without needing a perfectly quiet week to do it.

Is this personalised coaching?

Yes. The free Blueprint is a general starting point. 1-to-1 coaching adapts your training, nutrition and weekly goals to your circumstances and progress.

Do I need to train perfectly every week?

No. Plan for the week you have. That's the whole idea of the Hybrid Parent Plan: three full sessions when you can, shorter fallback workouts when you can't, and a routine you can maintain.

Is the Blueprint really free?

Yes. Open the calorie calculator, strength plan and weekly planner straight away. No signup required.
