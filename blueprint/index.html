01 / Eat well

Your nutrition starting point.

Estimate your daily calories with the free calculator, then use these foundations to build a routine you can repeat.

01

Start with the week you have

Record your usual meals for a week. Notice meal timing, portions, hunger and how training feels. Use this as a baseline rather than changing everything at once.

02

Give meals a simple structure

Include a protein source, fruit or vegetables, a carbohydrate source and some dietary fat. Plan a few repeatable breakfasts and lunches that fit your working day.

03

Review before you adjust

Look at consistency, energy, hunger and progress over several weeks. A nutrition target is a starting estimate and may need adjusting to your needs and goals.

General education for adults. Individual needs and medical dietary requirements need individual advice.

02 / Get strong

Three full-body sessions.

Use non-consecutive days. Warm up for 5–10 minutes and practise each movement with lighter resistance. Choose familiar, comfortable exercises and finish sets with 2–3 good repetitions left.

Start with 2 sets of 8–12 controlled repetitions per exercise. Rest 90–120 seconds. When all sets feel controlled at the top of the range, add the smallest available weight increase.

Short on time? Complete the first three exercises. Optional aerobic work: 20–30 minutes at a comfortable, conversational effort.

General starter framework. Adapt to experience and equipment; seek individual guidance for injury, pain or a health condition.
