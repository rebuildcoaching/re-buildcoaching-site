How this estimate works

We use the Mifflin–St Jeor equation to estimate resting energy expenditure, then apply an approximate activity multiplier (1.2, 1.375, 1.55 or 1.725). These activity categories are broad estimates, not measurements of your metabolism.

Our starting-point settings use 15% below estimated maintenance for fat loss, maintenance for weight stability, and 5% above maintenance for muscle gain alongside appropriate resistance training. Values are rounded to the nearest 50 kcal. These are coaching defaults, not personalised prescriptions or guaranteed results.

For low body weight or low calculated intakes, this tool directs you towards personalised advice instead of displaying a target. It is not designed for pregnancy, breastfeeding, under-18s, eating disorders or clinical nutrition needs. High-volume endurance training may need more tailored fuelling.

Read the original Mifflin–St Jeor research. For a more detailed weight-change model, see the NIDDK Body Weight Planner.
